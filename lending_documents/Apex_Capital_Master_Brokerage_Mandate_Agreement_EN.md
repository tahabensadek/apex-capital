## NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES HEREBY AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

### SECTION 1 — INDEPENDENT ORIGINATOR STATUS & NON-LENDER SCOPE
1.1. **Strict Origination Role:** The Client expressly acknowledges and agrees that Apex Capital acts solely as an independent commercial loan broker, financial packager, and origination intermediary. Apex Capital **is not a bank, not a depository institution, and not the direct lender**, nor is it a party to the underlying credit agreement between the Client and the funding lender (the "Lender").  
1.2. **Zero Loan Servicing Liability:** Apex Capital assumes no liability, obligation, or involvement regarding the administration, daily debit collections, loan terms, default management, or disputes arising between the Client and the Lender.  
1.3. **Exclusive Mandate Grant:** The Client hereby grants Apex Capital the **exclusive and irrevocable mandate** to prepare, package, underwrite, and present its credit file to its wholesale lender network for a term of ninety (90) days from the Effective Date.

---

### SECTION 2 — CONTINGENT SUCCESS FEE (7.00%) & MATURITY
2.1. **Zero Upfront Fees:** No retainer, application fee, file opening cost, or upfront expense of any kind is payable by the Client. If no funding is approved, accepted, or disbursed into the Client's bank account, the Client owes exactly **$0.00 CAD**.  
2.2. **Performance Success Fee:** In consideration for Apex Capital's structuring, financial analysis, packaging, and lender negotiation services, the Client irrevocably agrees to pay Apex Capital an advisory success fee equal to **seven percent (7.00%)** of the total gross principal capital disbursed or accepted.  
2.3. **Payment Maturity:** The 7.00% success fee is fully earned and legally payable by the Client **within a maximum of twenty-four (24) hours** following the clearance of the funding wire into the Client’s business bank account.

---

### SECTION 3 — PAYMENT TERMS & PRE-AUTHORIZED DEBIT (PAD / EFT) AUTHORIZATION
3.1. **Permitted Settlement Methods:** The Client agrees to remit the 7.00% fee using one of the following methods:  
   - **(a) Electronic Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD / EFT):** Initiated directly by Apex Capital;  
   - **(b) Corporate Wire or Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT):** Transmitted directly to Apex Capital's corporate treasury account;  
   - **(c) Corporate Interac e-Transfer:** Sent to `payments@apexcapital.ca`.  
3.2. **Formal Pre-Authorized Debit Authorization (Payments Canada Rule H1):**  
   The Client hereby formally, expressly, and irrevocably authorizes Apex Capital Inc. to electronically debit its commercial bank account (as verified via Plaid/Flinks open-banking credentials or attached void cheque) for the exact 7.00% fee amount upon confirmation that the lender’s gross loan proceeds have cleared into the account.  
   - *PAD Classification:* One-Time Business Pre-Authorized Debit (*Business PAD*).  
   - *Waiver of Pre-Notification:* The Client expressly waives any statutory pre-notification period to facilitate immediate same-day debit processing upon wire clearance.

---

### SECTION 4 — NON-CIRCUMVENTION & 24-MONTH TAIL PERIOD
4.1. **Non-Circumvention:** The Client agrees not to bypass, circumvent, or deal directly with any funding lender, syndicate, or underwriter introduced, contacted, or presented by Apex Capital during the term of this mandate.  
4.2. **24-Month Tail Period:** If, within **twenty-four (24) months** following the introduction of the file, the Client (or any affiliate, director, or related business entity) accepts or secures any financing facility, credit line increase, refinancing, or loan top-up from a lender introduced by Apex Capital, the **7.00% success fee** shall automatically and immediately be due and payable to Apex Capital.

---

### SECTION 5 — NOTIFICATION OF WIRE RECEIPT & DEFAULT
5.1. **Notice of Funding:** The Client agrees to notify Apex Capital via email or SMS within two (2) hours of the funding wire clearing into its bank account.  
5.2. **Default & Late Charges:** Any failure to settle the advisory fee within twenty-four (24) hours of loan clearance shall constitute a material breach of contract, resulting in:  
   - Accrual of late interest at the commercial legal rate of **19.80% per annum** (1.65% per month), calculated daily and compounded monthly;  
   - An immediate liquidated damages and collection indemnity equal to **twenty percent (20%) of the unpaid balance**, in addition to all actual legal fees and court costs incurred.

---
