## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 The Client appoints CapitalFacile to prepare its funding file and present it to one or more of CapitalFacile's partner funders (the "**Funder**") in order to obtain commercial funding of approximately **$`[AMOUNT]`** (the "**Funding**").

1.2 The Funding is exclusively for the Client's business purposes and not for personal, family or household purposes.

## 2. WHAT CAPITALFACILE IS AND IS NOT

2.1 CapitalFacile is an independent intermediary. **CapitalFacile is not a lender**, does not extend any funding and is not a party to the agreement between the Client and the Funder.

2.2 **CapitalFacile does not guarantee approval, the amount, the cost or the timing of any Funding.** The Funder alone reviews the application, decides whether to approve it, and sets the terms.

2.3 Funding is generally structured as a **purchase of part of the Client's future receivables** (merchant cash advance), not a loan. The Client acknowledges that it must read the Funder's agreement before signing it, that it is free to decline it, and that it may seek independent legal or accounting advice.

2.4 The Funder may require a personal guarantee from one or more of the Client's officers or shareholders.

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 This mandate is exclusive for **sixty (60) days** from signature. During that period, the Client agrees not to have the same request presented by another intermediary.

3.2 The Client may terminate this mandate at any time by written notice (email is sufficient). Termination does not affect section 5 (tail period).

## 4. SUCCESS FEE

4.1 **No upfront fees.** No application, review or other fees are payable.

4.2 **Fee.** If, and only if, (a) the Client signs a funding agreement with a Funder to which CapitalFacile presented its file **and** (b) funds are deposited into the Client's account, the Client pays CapitalFacile a fee equal to **six percent (6%) of the gross Funding amount**, meaning the total amount advanced or purchased by the Funder before any deduction of the Funder's own fees, plus applicable taxes, if any.

   *Example: on $50,000 of Funding, the fee is $3,000.*

4.3 **No fee** is owed if the Funding is not approved, if the Client declines the offer, or if no funds are deposited.

4.4 **Due date.** The fee is due **two (2) business days** after the funds are deposited into the Client's account.

4.5 **Paid directly.** The fee is paid **by the Client directly to CapitalFacile**. The Funder plays no part in invoicing or collecting it, and it is not part of the agreement with the Funder.

4.6 **Refund.** If the funding agreement is cancelled and the Client returns all funds received to the Funder within five (5) business days of their deposit, CapitalFacile refunds its fee.

4.7 **Late payment.** Any amount unpaid when due bears interest at **1% per month (12% per year)**. The Client bears reasonable legal costs of collection, if any.

## 5. TAIL PERIOD

5.1 The fee under section 4.2 is also owed if, within **six (6) months** after its file is presented, the Client (or a related company) obtains funding from a Funder to which CapitalFacile presented that file, whether during or after the mandate.

## 6. COMPENSATION DISCLOSURE

6.1 The Client is informed and agrees that **CapitalFacile may also receive compensation from the Funder** when Funding is granted, in addition to the fee under this mandate.
